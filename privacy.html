Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This page explains what information collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, so we can send you a receipt and help with your order
  • The mobile money number you pay with, so we can match a payment to your order
  • Details of your order, such as which book you bought and when

What we do not see

We never see or store your mobile money PIN. You enter it on your own phone.

How we use your information

We use it to complete your purchase, let you download your book, answer your questions and prevent fraud. We do not sell your information.

Who else handles it

Payments are processed by our payment provider. Our website and order records are hosted on Google Firebase. These services receive only what they need to do their job.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to support your purchase, resolve problems and meet our legal obligations.
