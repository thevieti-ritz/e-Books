Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Because ebooks are digital files that you can download immediately, we cannot take them back once they have been delivered. We still want you to be treated fairly.

When we will refund you

  • You were charged but did not receive a working download
  • The file is damaged or is not the book that was described
  • You were charged twice for the same book

When we cannot refund you

  • You changed your mind after downloading the book
  • You bought the wrong book by mistake and have already downloaded it

How to ask for a refund

Email within 7 days of your purchase. Include the mobile money number you paid with, the date and time, and the name of the book. Refunds are sent back to the mobile money number used for the payment.
